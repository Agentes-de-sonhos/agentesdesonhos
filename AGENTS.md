# Architecture rules
- Magic Kingdom maps mount on demand in an accessible same-origin iframe; image URLs use CDN pointers and Escape validates origin and source. Keep the approved width-based map and natural sidebar flow; measure the iframe content height for its original desktop/mobile viewport proportions. Why: preserves the approved presentation and quote behavior without a height-driven contain redesign.
- The Orlando editorial photo gallery is composed at the public tickets route only for an explicit hostname allowlist (Destinos com a Ju, 100 Limites), outside the shared wizard; its photographs use CDN pointers with official-source provenance. Why: preserves all wizard behavior, home rendering and other tenants.

- White-label hero visual variants must be declared in the tenant profile and resolved by the shared home renderer with safe defaults, preserving tenant isolation.
- White-label photo-above campaign cards and personal visited-destination strips are optional profile fields rendered by shared components, off by default; preview-only finishing stays scoped to its theme class. Why: personalizes one fixture without duplicating the engine or changing other tenants.
- ADS briefing e-mail: server-to-server Edge Function `ads-briefing-email` with own relay-token auth (SHA-256 in private `ads_briefing_integration`), private service-role-only queue + production-run tables; retries piggyback on the `product-landing-lead-emails` cron. Why: owner-only internal production trigger, no public/tenant surface.
- ADS site previews use path-keyed local fixtures on synthetic hostnames and the shared white-label renderer, gated to technical preview hosts with all commercial actions and backend access blocked. Why: enables review without publishing or touching tenant data.
- Sale contract templates may embed dynamic fields inside fixed legal text via `agency_contract_templates.render_config` (mode `slotted`, tokens resolved by `src/lib/contractSlots.ts`); literal agency text lives in `src/lib/contractTemplates/*`. Why: one generic contract engine for every agency, with the legal text left exactly as written and no per-agency hard-coding.
- SiteLab Base commercial presentation (section/theme selection + prospect identity) is stored per browser in localStorage (`src/lib/sitelabPresentation.ts`) and edited at `/sitelab-base/gestao/apresentacao`, applied only when `profile.demo`. Why: `sitelab_templates` writes are platform-admin-only and the lab panel rejects admins, so DB persistence would need a permission expansion.
- Official US B1/B2 visa data lives in the single-row public table `us_visa_public_info` (updated externally by a scheduled ChatGPT task, no in-app cron) and is consumed only opt-in via `useUsVisaPublicInfo` / `UsVisaOfficialInfo`. Why: one shared official source for all agency sites without auto-enabling it anywhere.
- The 100 Limites DMC portfolio is one landing (`/dmc-portugal`, `DmcPortugalLandingPage`) routed only for the `editorialDmc` profile; legacy portfolio paths redirect to its anchors in `AgencyDomainRoutes` while their declarative content stays in `agencySiteContentPages`. Why: single B2B page without breaking old links or touching other tenants.
- Site → Blog is an opt-in per-agency module gated by entitlement `site_blog` + team permission `site.blog.manage` (RLS `blog_can_manage`); status/published versions change only through SECURITY DEFINER `blog_post_action`/`blog_run_scheduled`, and public reads go only through the `public-blog` Edge Function (published or due-scheduled snapshot, private media signed per own post). Why: strong tenant isolation and no partial autosave leaking to visitors.
- ADS previews may expose optional internal pages (Xcaret, resorts, Orlando tickets) declared per fixture under `pages`, routed inside `/ads-briefing-preview/:jobId/*` with commercial actions still blocked. Why: reviews full sites without touching the real tenants' routes.
